# Remove Nestor

**What this does:** Uninstall Nestor completely.

## Before you start
- Cancel your subscription first if you don't want to be charged again (see *Manage your subscription*).

## Steps
1. **Settings → Devices & services → Nestor → ⋮ → Delete**.
2. **Settings → Marketplace → Downloaded → Nestor AI Butler → ⋮ → Remove**.
3. Restart Home Assistant.
4. Optional: **Settings → Voice assistants** → choose another assistant as **Preferred**, then delete the Nestor one.

## How you know it worked
Nestor is gone from the sidebar and from Devices & services. Dashboards Nestor created stay — delete them in Settings → Dashboards if you don't want them.

## If it didn't work
| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| The Nestor panel is still in the sidebar | Refresh the browser page. |

Still stuck? Email **support@your-domain.com** with a screenshot of what you see.
