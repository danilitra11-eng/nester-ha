# Create automations by asking

**What this does:** Make things happen automatically without the automation editor.

## Before you start
- Family or Pro plan.
- **⚙️ Create automations** is ticked in permissions.

## Steps
1. Ask, for example: "every weekday at 7 open the bedroom blinds" or "turn the porch light on at sunset".
2. Nestor confirms what it set up. It appears in **Settings → Automations** with "(created by Nestor)".
   > 📷 *Screenshot: automation in the list*
3. To pause or delete: "pause the porch light automation" / "delete it" (Nestor asks you to confirm deleting).
4. Nestor also suggests automations on Sundays when it notices a routine. Tap **Yes, automate it** to accept.
   > 📷 *Screenshot: suggestion message*

## How you know it worked
The automation runs at the time you asked.

## If it didn't work
| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| "would control 'lock', which I'm not allowed" | Automations can only do what Nestor itself may do. Change permissions first. |
| "doesn't keep automations in automations.yaml" | Your configuration is customised; create it in Settings → Automations instead. |

Still stuck? Email **support@your-domain.com** with a screenshot of what you see.
