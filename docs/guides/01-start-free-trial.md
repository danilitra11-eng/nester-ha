# Start your free trial

**What this does:** Get a Nestor license key, free for 7 days.

## Before you start
- An email address and a payment card. You won't be charged during the 7 days, and you can cancel any time before that.

## Steps
1. Go to **your-domain.com** and select **Start free trial** on the plan you want (Starter or Family).
   > 📷 *Screenshot: pricing section with the two plans*
2. Enter your email and card details, then select **Start trial**.
   > 📷 *Screenshot: checkout page*
3. Open the email from Nestor with the subject **Your Nestor license key**. Keep it — you'll paste the key in a moment.
   > 📷 *Screenshot: the email with the key highlighted*

## How you know it worked
You have an email with a key that looks like `XXXX-XXXX-XXXX`.

## If it didn't work
| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| No email after 5 minutes | Check your spam or promotions folder. Search your inbox for "Nestor". |
| Card was declined | Try another card, or contact your bank. Nestor can't see your card details. |

Still stuck? Email **support@your-domain.com** with a screenshot of what you see.
