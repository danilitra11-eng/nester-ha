# Restart Home Assistant

**What this does:** Load newly installed integrations like Nestor.

## Before you start
- Nothing — restarting is safe. Your devices keep working; Home Assistant is back in about a minute.

## Steps
1. Open **Settings**.
2. Select **⋮** (top right) → **Restart Home Assistant**.
   > 📷 *Screenshot: restart menu*
3. Select **Restart** and wait about a minute until the page comes back.

## How you know it worked
The page reloads and you're back on the Home Assistant dashboard.

## If it didn't work
| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| The page says it's unavailable for more than 5 minutes | Unplug the Home Assistant device, wait 10 seconds and plug it back in. |

Still stuck? Email **support@your-domain.com** with a screenshot of what you see.
