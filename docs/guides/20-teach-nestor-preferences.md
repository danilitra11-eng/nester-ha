# Teach Nestor your preferences

**What this does:** Nestor remembers what you like and uses it later.

## Before you start
- Family or Pro plan.

## Steps
1. Tell Nestor, in any way you talk to it: "I like the bedroom at 70 at night", "the dog is called Bruno", "I wake up at 6:30 on weekdays".
2. Nestor confirms it'll remember.
3. To see or delete memories: **Nestor** → **⚙︎ Settings** → **What Nestor remembers** → **Forget**. Or just say "forget that…".
   > 📷 *Screenshot: memory card*

## How you know it worked
Later, "make the bedroom comfy" sets it to 70 without you saying the number.

## If it didn't work
| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| Nestor didn't remember | Say it as a lasting preference ("I always…", "I like…"). One-off requests aren't saved. |

Still stuck? Email **support@your-domain.com** with a screenshot of what you see.
