# Change settings just by asking

**What this does:** Adjust Nestor without menus.

## Before you start
- Nothing.

## Steps
1. Say or type any of these in Nestor, the phone app or Telegram:
2. "Call yourself Max." · "Be more casual." · "Be brief." · "Answer in Spanish." · "Read your replies aloud." · "Quiet hours from 10 PM to 7 AM." · "Turn off quiet hours."
3. Nestor confirms the change. It applies from your next message.

## How you know it worked
Nestor confirms, and its next answers follow the new setting.

## If it didn't work
| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| Nestor says it can't change permissions | That's on purpose, for safety. See *Change what Nestor may control*. |

Still stuck? Email **support@your-domain.com** with a screenshot of what you see.
