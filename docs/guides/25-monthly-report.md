# Your monthly report

**What this does:** See what Nestor did for you and the estimated energy savings.

## Before you start
- Nothing.

## Steps
1. On the 1st of each month Nestor sends a summary. After your first 5 days you also get a short "first days" summary.
2. Anytime: **Nestor** → **🏠 Home** → **This month**, or ask "what did you do this month?"
   > 📷 *Screenshot: report card*

## How you know it worked
You see requests handled, things done, alerts, problems caught, automations and estimated savings.

## If it didn't work
| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| Savings look off | Savings are estimates, based on $0.17 per kWh. A setting for your own electricity price is coming soon. |

Still stuck? Email **support@your-domain.com** with a screenshot of what you see.
