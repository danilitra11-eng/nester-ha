# Manage your subscription

**What this does:** Upgrade, change your card, or cancel.

## Before you start
- The receipt email from your first payment.

## Steps
1. Open any receipt email from Nestor and select **Manage subscription**.
   > 📷 *Screenshot: receipt email*
2. Choose **Change plan**, **Update payment method** or **Cancel subscription**.
   > 📷 *Screenshot: customer portal*
3. Confirm.

## How you know it worked
You get a confirmation email. After cancelling, Nestor keeps working until the end of the period you paid for.

## If it didn't work
| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| Can't find the receipt email | Email support from the address you subscribed with and we'll send you a link. |

Still stuck? Email **support@your-domain.com** with a screenshot of what you see.
