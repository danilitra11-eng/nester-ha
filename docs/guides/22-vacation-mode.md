# Vacation mode

**What this does:** Make the house look lived-in while you're away.

## Before you start
- Pro plan.
- Lights are allowed in permissions.

## Steps
1. Say "we're going away for a week" and agree when Nestor offers vacation mode, or open **Nestor** → **🏠 Home** → tick **Vacation mode**.
   > 📷 *Screenshot: vacation card*
2. While it's on, lights come and go between sunset and 11 PM (not in bedrooms), and everything is off overnight.
3. When you're back: "we're home" or untick it.

## How you know it worked
In the evening, you'll see lights change in Home Assistant's logbook.

## If it didn't work
| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| No lights change | Check that lights are allowed in permissions and that it's after sunset. |

Still stuck? Email **support@your-domain.com** with a screenshot of what you see.
