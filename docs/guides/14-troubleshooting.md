# Troubleshooting

**What this does:** Fix the most common messages.

## Before you start
- Open **Nestor** in the sidebar — the line under the name shows your plan or the problem.

## Steps
1. Find the message you see in the table below and follow its fix.

## How you know it worked
The status line under Nestor's name shows your plan and how much is left this month.

## If it didn't work
| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Your license key isn't valid" | Settings → Devices & services → Nestor → ⋮ → Delete, then set it up again with the key from your email. |
| "Your subscription isn't active" | Renew from the link in the message, or see *Manage your subscription*. |
| "You've reached this month's limit" | Upgrade to Family, or wait — limits reset on the 1st of each month. |
| "I can't reach the server" | Check Home Assistant's internet connection. If it's fine, Nestor may be briefly down — try again in a few minutes. |
| Nestor uses the wrong device | Give devices clear names and put them in rooms (Settings → Areas), or ask Nestor to do it: "rename switch 3 to boiler". |
| Nestor doesn't speak | Check *Read replies aloud* in ⚙︎ Settings and that you're not in quiet hours. |

Still stuck? Email **support@your-domain.com** with a screenshot of what you see.
