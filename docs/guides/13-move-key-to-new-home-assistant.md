# Move your key to a new Home Assistant

**What this does:** Use your subscription on a new or reinstalled Home Assistant.

## Before you start
- Your license key.

## Steps
1. On the old Home Assistant (if you still have it): **Settings → Devices & services → Nestor → ⋮ → Delete**.
2. Email **support@your-domain.com** from your subscription email and ask to free your key. We reply within one business day.
3. On the new Home Assistant, follow *Install Nestor* and *Set up Nestor*.

## How you know it worked
The setup wizard accepts your key.

## If it didn't work
| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| "already used on the maximum number of homes" | Support hasn't freed the key yet — reply to their email. |

Still stuck? Email **support@your-domain.com** with a screenshot of what you see.
