# Choose your alerts and where they go

**What this does:** Decide what Nestor tells you about on its own, and on which phones.

## Before you start
- You're an administrator in Home Assistant.
- Optional: the Home Assistant app on your phone, logged in to your home, and/or Telegram connected.

## Steps
1. Open **Nestor** in the sidebar → **🏠 Home** → **🔔 Alerts**.
   > 📷 *Screenshot: alerts card*
2. Tick the alerts you want. Leaks, smoke, gas and CO are always sent right away, even in quiet hours.
3. Under **Send to these phones**, tick the phones that should get alerts.
4. Select **Save**, then **Send a test**.

## How you know it worked
Your phone (and Telegram, if connected) shows "👋 Test from Nestor".

## If it didn't work
| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| No phones listed | Install the Home Assistant app on the phone and log in to your home; it appears here a minute later. |
| Test didn't arrive on the phone | On the phone: Settings → Notifications → Home Assistant → allow notifications. |
| Some alerts are locked 🔒 | They're part of the Family or Pro plan. See *Manage your subscription*. |

Still stuck? Email **support@your-domain.com** with a screenshot of what you see.
