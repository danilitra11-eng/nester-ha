# Choose and preview Nestor's voice

**What this does:** Pick the voice Nestor uses when it talks.

## Before you start
- You're an administrator in Home Assistant.

## Steps
1. Open **Nestor** in the sidebar and select **⚙︎ Settings**.
   > 📷 *Screenshot: Nestor settings tab*
2. In **Voice**, select **▶** next to any voice to hear a sample.
   > 📷 *Screenshot: voice list*
3. Select **Select** next to the voice you like.

## How you know it worked
The voice shows **Selected**, and the next spoken reply uses it.

## If it didn't work
| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| Only one or two voices in the list | Add more free voices: **Settings → Devices & services → + Add integration → Piper** (choose voices for your language). Home Assistant Cloud also adds natural voices. |
| ▶ makes no sound | Check your computer's or phone's volume, and that the browser tab isn't muted. |

Still stuck? Email **support@your-domain.com** with a screenshot of what you see.
