# Home check-up

**What this does:** Find devices that stopped working, low batteries and broken integrations.

## Before you start
- Family or Pro plan.

## Steps
1. Nestor checks every morning and messages you only if something new is wrong.
2. To check now: **Nestor** → **🏠 Home** → **Home check-up** → **Check now**, or ask "is everything OK at home?"
   > 📷 *Screenshot: health card*
3. For a broken integration, tap **Restart …** in the message.

## How you know it worked
The card shows "✓ Everything looks fine", or lists what needs attention.

## If it didn't work
| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| A device keeps showing as not responding | Check its power or batteries, then restart it from its device page in Settings → Devices. |

Still stuck? Email **support@your-domain.com** with a screenshot of what you see.
