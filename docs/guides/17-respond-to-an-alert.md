# Respond to an alert

**What this does:** Fix what Nestor noticed with one tap.

## Before you start
- An alert from Nestor on your phone or in Telegram.

## Steps
1. Read the alert, for example "Garage door has been open for 34 minutes and nobody's home."
   > 📷 *Screenshot: alert with buttons*
2. Tap the action button (for example **Close it**) to do it, **Not now** to be left alone about it for a while, or **✖** to ignore.
3. Nestor replies with what it did.

## How you know it worked
The device changes (the garage closes) and Nestor confirms.

## If it didn't work
| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| "That button has expired" | Buttons work for 6 hours. Just tell Nestor what you want instead. |
| "not allowed" | That device type isn't allowed. See *Change what Nestor may control*. |

Still stuck? Email **support@your-domain.com** with a screenshot of what you see.
