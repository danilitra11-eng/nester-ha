# Cameras and the doorbell

**What this does:** Ask what a camera sees, and get a description when the doorbell rings.

## Before you start
- Pro plan.
- **📷 Look through cameras** is ticked in permissions.
- Cameras already work in Home Assistant.

## Steps
1. Ask: "is there a package at the front door?" or "is the garage empty?"
2. For doorbell descriptions, keep **🔔 Doorbell with camera description** ticked in **🏠 Home → Alerts**. Nestor uses the camera in the same room as the doorbell (or one with "door" or "front" in its name).

## How you know it worked
When the doorbell rings, you get "🔔 Someone's at the door: …".

## If it didn't work
| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Someone rang the doorbell" without a description | Put the doorbell and the camera in the same area (Settings → Areas). |
| Nestor says it can't look | Tick **📷 Look through cameras** in permissions. |

Still stuck? Email **support@your-domain.com** with a screenshot of what you see.
