# Talk to Nestor from your phone, watch or speaker

**What this does:** Use Nestor anywhere, not just in the browser.

## Before you start
- The Home Assistant app on your phone, logged in to your home.
- Nestor is your preferred voice assistant (previous guide).

## Steps
1. **Phone:** open the Home Assistant app and tap **Assist** (💬, top right), then type or tap 🎤 and speak.
   > 📷 *Screenshot: Assist on phone*
2. **Phone home screen (optional):** add the Assist widget or shortcut from the app's settings for one-tap access.
3. **Watch:** open the Home Assistant app on your watch and tap **Assist**.
4. **Voice speaker:** just say the wake word, then your request.

## How you know it worked
Nestor answers, and the device does what you asked.

## If it didn't work
| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Sorry, I didn't understand" in a different voice | Your phone is using another assistant: in Assist, tap the assistant name at the top and choose Nestor. |

Still stuck? Email **support@your-domain.com** with a screenshot of what you see.
