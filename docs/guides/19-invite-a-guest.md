# Give a guest temporary access

**What this does:** A babysitter, dog sitter or neighbour can use Nestor on Telegram for a limited time, with only the basics.

## Before you start
- You're an administrator in Home Assistant.
- Pro plan.

## Steps
1. Open **Nestor** → **⚙︎ Settings** → **Family & guests** → **Invite a guest**.
   > 📷 *Screenshot: invite form*
2. Type their name, choose how many hours of access, and select **Create guest code**.
3. Send the guest the code and the bot name shown. They open Telegram, find the bot, tap **Start**, and send the code.

## How you know it worked
The guest gets "Welcome! You have guest access to this home until…". They appear under *Connected on Telegram* as a guest.

## If it didn't work
| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| You want to end access early | Select **Remove** next to their name. |
| Guest asks to unlock doors or change settings | That's by design: guests can only use lights, climate, TV and blinds. |

Still stuck? Email **support@your-domain.com** with a screenshot of what you see.
