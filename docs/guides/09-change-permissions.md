# Change what Nestor may control

**What this does:** Allow or block device types, home-structure editing and dashboard design.

## Before you start
- You're an administrator in Home Assistant.

## Steps
1. Open **Nestor** in the sidebar → **⚙︎ Settings** → **Permissions**.
   > 📷 *Screenshot: permissions card*
2. Tick or untick items. **🏠 Edit rooms, floors & names** and **🎨 Dashboards & themes** let Nestor change your setup, always asking before it deletes anything.
3. Select **Save**.
4. Same settings are also in **Settings → Devices & services → Nestor → Configure**.

## How you know it worked
Ask Nestor to do something you just blocked — it says it isn't allowed.

## If it didn't work
| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| The checkboxes are greyed out | Only Home Assistant administrators can change permissions. Ask the person who set up Home Assistant. |

Still stuck? Email **support@your-domain.com** with a screenshot of what you see.
